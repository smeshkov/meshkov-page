<p>I was born in Zelenogorsk, a small town in the middle of Russia&apos;s Siberia — roughly, Green Mountains, if you tried to translate it. A few moves and a family later, I live in Sydney, Australia.</p>

<p>Software engineer by trade, photographer and videographer by passion, surfer at heart.</p>

<p>This site is my personal space: somewhere to put the photographs and films, and to write about how they get made. Mostly photography, sometimes surfing, occasionally software.</p>
